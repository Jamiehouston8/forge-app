PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: JUNE 2026

1. WHO WE ARE

Forge ("we", "us", "our") is a gamified personal development app available at forges.fit. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at support@forges.fit.

2. WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect the following information when you use Forge:

  • Account information — your email address and password when you sign up
  • Profile data — your username, goals, health stats, workout logs and any other content you add to the app
  • Onboarding answers — information you provide during setup (age, fitness level, goals etc.) used to personalise your experience
  • Usage data — how you interact with the app, XP earned, streaks, sessions completed
  • Payment information — billing is handled securely by Stripe. We do not store your card details. We receive confirmation of payment status only.
  • Device information — browser type, device type and IP address for security and performance purposes

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use your information to:

  • Provide and personalise the Forge app experience
  • Process your subscription payments via Stripe
  • Maintain your account and sync your data across devices
  • Show your ranking on the leaderboard (using your chosen username only)
  • Send important account notifications (password resets, billing updates)
  • Improve the app based on how it is used

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

4. DATA STORAGE

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL), hosted on servers within the European Union. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed within 30 days.

5. THIRD PARTY SERVICES

Forge uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (privacy policy)
  • Stripe — payment processing (privacy policy)
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — AI mentor responses (privacy policy)
  • Netlify — website hosting (privacy policy)

6. COOKIES

Forge uses minimal cookies and local storage to keep you logged in and save your app preferences. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Under UK and EU data protection law (GDPR/UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Object to how we process your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@forges.fit.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Forge is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or via the app. The date at the top of this page reflects when it was last updated.
