COMMON QUESTIONS

How do I delete my account?
Open Forge, go to Account, and choose Delete Account at the bottom. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account and all your data are permanently removed.

I forgot my password.
Tap "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a reset link. If it doesn't arrive, email us from the address you signed up with.

Is the AI mentor giving professional advice?
No. Mentor replies are AI-generated suggestions for building habits and hitting goals. They aren't medical, financial or legal advice.
